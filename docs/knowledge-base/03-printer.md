# My document will not print

**Audience:** Employees · **Category:** Printing

## Try these steps

1. Check that the selected printer is the intended one for your location.
2. Check the printer display for an error, paper shortage or visible jam. Follow posted instructions and do not open restricted equipment panels.
3. Check whether your own print job is paused or waiting. Avoid resubmitting many copies.
4. If you can cancel your own stuck job, do so once and retry a small nonconfidential test page.
5. Ask whether another employee can print to the same device.

## Contact IT when

The issue persists, several people are affected, or a production label printer is involved. Do not stop shared print services or delete other users' jobs.

Include the printer name/location, your asset ID, displayed error, application and whether the problem affects other users. Do not attach confidential documents as examples.
