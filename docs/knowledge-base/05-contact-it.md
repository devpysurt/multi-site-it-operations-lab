# Send a useful IT support request

**Audience:** Employees · **Category:** Getting help

Use the company's approved support channel. This fictional lab deliberately does not invent a real helpdesk email or phone number.

## Include

- A short subject: what is failing and where.
- Your site and the device asset ID.
- When the issue started, including timezone if relevant.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- The exact error or a redacted screenshot.
- Whether one user, several users or a whole location is affected.
- Steps already tried and a way to contact you.
- Business impact: blocked task, workaround and urgency.

## Example

> Subject: Reykjanes — LAB-REY-001 cannot print to the office printer
>
> Since 09:15 local time, my document stays in the queue. A colleague can print. I checked the selected printer and cancelled my own job once. Other applications work. I can use the second office printer temporarily. Please contact me through the approved support channel.

## Escalate promptly

Use the organization's urgent incident route for a site-wide outage, suspected security incident or interruption affecting production. Do not wait for a routine ticket if the local emergency procedure says otherwise. Never include passwords, MFA codes, tokens or confidential files.
