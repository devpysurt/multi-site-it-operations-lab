# Workstation handover checklist

## Before handover

- [ ] Record fictional or authorized asset identifier, serial, site and assigned employee.
- [ ] Review requested apps and the workstation configuration.
- [ ] Generate and inspect a setup preview.
- [ ] Apply approved setup and inspect the journal for failures or declined actions.
- [ ] Run diagnostics; resolve or document low disk space and unavailable data.
- [ ] Confirm basic network, printer and application behavior with the user.
- [ ] Confirm sign-in and access through the approved identity process (manual in v0.1).
- [ ] Provide the support contact and knowledge-base instructions.
